About MindScore

MindScore is a machine-learning-based application that estimates a mental health score from lifestyle, academic, stress, and social-media usage information.

  • It's an educational machine-learning project, built to demonstrate a trained prediction model in a real interface.
  • It does not diagnose any mental health condition.
  • Its predictions should never replace a professional evaluation — please speak with a qualified professional for any concerns about your wellbeing.
